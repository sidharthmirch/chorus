Transformer architecture

#concept #architectures from _templates/concept.md
Definition

Sequence model mapping tokens through stacked [[attention]] and MLP blocks. Modern production variants add [[mixture-of-experts]] routing, [[rotary-embeddings]], and a [[kv-cache]] for autoregressive decoding.

Why it matters here

Inference economics of this architecture (memory-bound decode, KV-cache size) drive the HBM demand analyzed in [[NVIDIA]] and [[HBM memory]] — it's the causal link between model design and semiconductor revenue.
